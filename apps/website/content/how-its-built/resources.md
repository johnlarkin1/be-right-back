<!--SECTION:papers:Papers-->

The primary sources. If you want the math instead of my hand-waving:

- **[HNSW — Malkov & Yashunin](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.09320)** — the graph index our search pipeline used for approximate nearest neighbours, until we measured it and switched to an exact scan.
- **[InfoNCE — van den Oord et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.03748)** — the contrastive objective that makes "dinner plans" and "what are we eating tonight" land near each other in vector space.
- **[The Probabilistic Relevance Framework: BM25 and Beyond — Robertson & Zaragoza](https://www.staff.city.ac.uk/~sb317/papers/foundations_bm25_review.pdf)** — the definitive treatment of BM25, the lexical half of hybrid search.
- **[Reciprocal Rank Fusion — Cormack et al.](https://plg.uwaterloo.ca/~gvcormac/cormacksigir09-rrf.pdf)** — the absurdly simple algorithm that merges semantic + BM25 rankings without normalizing scores.
- **[A Tutorial on Principal Component Analysis — Shlens](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.1100)** — the cleanest PCA write-up if you want it on paper.
- **[When Is "Nearest Neighbor" Meaningful? — Beyer et al. (1999)](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/3-540-49257-7_15)** — the curse-of-dimensionality result that forces us to reduce dimensions *before* clustering.

Blog posts, vendor explainers, and docs that were directly useful:

- **[Contextual Retrieval](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/contextual-retrieval)** — Anthropic. Why you still want BM25 alongside embeddings in 2026.
- **[HNSW explainer](https://www.pinecone.io/learn/series/faiss/hnsw/)** — Pinecone. The approachable version of the paper above.
- **[The BM25 Algorithm and its Variables](https://www.elastic.co/blog/practical-bm25-part-2-the-bm25-algorithm-and-its-variables)** — Elastic. `k1` and `b`, demystified.
- **[Maximal Marginal Relevance](https://medium.com/@adergunov/maximal-marginal-relevance-144c23b42be5)** — the dedup/diversity step in the search pipeline.
- **[Replacement Scans](https://duckdb.org/docs/current/clients/c/replacement_scans)** — DuckDB. How `SELECT * FROM df` scans a DataFrame in place with zero copies.
- **[UMAP docs](https://umap-learn.readthedocs.io/en/latest/)** — the canonical reference, including the Riemannian-manifold assumptions I admittedly still don't fully grok.
- **[Building an agentic loop](https://docs.temporal.io/ai-cookbook/agentic-loop-tool-call-claude-python)** — Temporal's cookbook, the shape [Casper](/how-its-built/casper-agent) borrows.
- **[XML Tool Calls](https://docs.morphllm.com/guides/xml-tool-calls)** — Morph. Context on why Qwen3.5 moved tool calls from JSON to XML.
- **[A Walk in the Parquet](https://johnlarkin1.github.io/2025/walk-in-the-parquet/)** & **[Into the Arena](https://johnlarkin1.github.io/2024/into-the-arena/)** — my own earlier write-ups on the Parquet format and hierarchical clustering, both of which fed directly into this project.
